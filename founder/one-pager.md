# Site Manager · one page

**Verdict: Not yet** (computed): unit economics pass; year 1 with founder pay and the simulated buy rate fail.

**The problem.** A Sofia café's prices, hours and daily special change every week, but its website, Google listing and QR menu stay wrong, because the owner has no web person and no time. Only 47.4% of Bulgarian food and accommodation businesses with 10+ staff have a website (EU: 76.5%).

**The customer.** Owner-operators of independent cafés, bars and small restaurants in Sofia: 2,742 categorised venues, ~98% independent; 22,756 food-service venues in Bulgaria.

**The offer and price.** Message the agent ("cappuccino is 3.20 now", a voice note, a photo of the board), tap once, and it is live. **Website once:** keep your own site 0 €, Ready site 199 €. **Agent monthly:** Menu 17 € (site + QR, by chat with text and voice), **Everywhere 30 €** (plus photos, Google hours, menu and posts, and today's special posted to Instagram). Promo: website + one year paid up front = 2 months free. Excl. VAT; keep your domain if you leave.

**The evidence.**
- Real: four Sofia venues quoted 20, 30, 50 and 70 €/month (conversations, not contracts); the product is live and one venue (Coffique) runs its menu from it.
- Simulated panel (20 owners): 2 buy (10%). 12 of 18 no's would flip if one message also fixed Google (and delivery apps).
- Competitor gap: chat-edited sites exist (Clawfront, bot2site) but none are in Bulgarian or venue-specific; BG menu vendors (3–60 €) are admin-driven and menu-only.

**What the money is for.** About 500 € of setup (company, legal templates, QR stands, visits). The rest is the founder's time, which decides whether this needs 616 € or 8,984 €.

**The ask / next step.** Google Business Profile API access applied for this week; 10 venue visits testing 17 € "Menu" against 30 € "Everywhere"; target 3 paying venues within 60 days.
