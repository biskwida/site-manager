# Pitch notes

Points to use in the deck and in Q&A, collected 2026-10-11. Numbers come from the founder pack (`business-plan.md`, `pricing.md`, `cfo.md`, `competitors.md`, `expansion.md`).

## The main message

**"You no longer need a person to keep your website up to date."**
No web developer, agency, relative or staff member to call and pay for every change. The owner sends a message, a voice note or a photo, and the site updates itself (Instagram too, and Google next).
- Today: Sofia agencies charge 250–500 € to build a site, then **51–256 € a month** for upkeep.
- Site Manager: **199 € once, then 17–30 € a month, with changes included.**

## Our edge: one agent runs the venue's whole online presence

Nobody we found does all of this together:

1. **We build the website** for you (pick a design, menu read from a photo, **photos imported straight from your Instagram**, live the same day).
2. **You run it from whichever app you're comfortable with:** Telegram, WhatsApp or Messenger, by text, voice note or photo.
3. **The same message posts to your Instagram,** as a feed post or a **story** (today's special, the daily menu).
4. **Already have a WordPress site? Keep it.** Our plugin puts the menu, specials and hours on the site you already have, and you run them from the same chat. Nobody else we found does this: Clawfront and bot2site only run their own sites, and WordPress menu plugins (QRMenu Lite, PlugTide, MenuMaster) are edited in the WordPress admin, without chat or AI.
5. **Next: Google Business** (hours, holiday hours, menu). It isn't in the demo, but it's the same idea: **one agent to control your business online**: site, Instagram and Google from one chat.

Against the rivals:
- **Clawfront** (closest): builds a site and edits it from **WhatsApp only**; no Instagram posting, no stories, no restaurant features found in our research.
- **bot2site:** Telegram only, no AI.
- **Bulgarian QR-menu apps:** a menu edited in an app, not your site, not your social media.

**What it does today:** website + wizard, Telegram, WhatsApp, Messenger, preview and undo, price guard, **Instagram posts and stories from the chat**. **Next:** a Viber chatbot (many owners run their day on Viber and won't install another app; one bot, about 100 € a month, serves every venue) and Google Business.

## Who it's for

**Every café, bar and restaurant** whose prices, hours, sold-out items or specials change. **Venues with a daily menu, or that cook every day, get the most out of it** (lunch menus, daily specials, bistros, home-style restaurants): their menu changes every morning, so a photo of the board or a voice note each day is the core use.
- Evidence: in both buyer-panel runs (20 simulated owners each), the only 2 buyers were daily-menu owners ("my lunch special changes every single day").

## What it does (demo order)

1. A voice note or text: "cappuccino is 3.20 now" → preview → one tap → live.
2. A photo of the specials board → the AI reads it and updates today's specials.
3. **Price guard:** a misread price (4.50 → 14.50) gets a warning and a second "Is this right?" before it goes live.
4. Undo any change, scheduled changes, sold-out items that come back automatically.
5. **From the same message:** **today's special posted to the venue's Instagram, as a feed post or story** (the owner chooses per post). **Next:** Google hours and menu. Facebook Page posting: maybe later, not promised. A daily menu as a morning Instagram story fits the daily-menu venues exactly. 4 of 20 simulated owners asked for social posting; most of the others asked for Google.

## Likely questions

**"Do you have competition?" / "Isn't this just Clawfront or bot2site?"** Nobody does the whole combination: a Bulgarian, venue-specific site run by chat from the apps the owner already uses, with AI, preview and undo. But parts of it exist:
- **Clawfront** (closest): WhatsApp, understands text and voice with AI, has a restaurants page; $4.99/month plus AI credits. Generic, English-first, no sold-out items, specials or change history.
- **bot2site**: Telegram only (its own line: the only builder that "lives entirely in Telegram"), a guided bot with **no AI** ("templates, not AI chaos"), 50+ one-page templates for any business. Free; 11.99 €/month with your own domain.
- **ChilledSites**: builds a site from one WhatsApp/Telegram message, £9/month.
- **Bulgarian QR-menu vendors** (qr-menu.bg says 247+ venues, digitalnomenu.bg, 1MENU, e-menu.bg; 3–60 €/month; doXmenu from Skopje now also targets Bulgaria): a menu edited in an app, not the venue's site. The fastest potential copiers: they already have the venues and the menus.
- **The biggest competitor is free:** Instagram, Facebook, Google's own menu, a PDF, or nothing. Most "no" answers in the simulated owner panel were "I post the specials on Facebook for free".

**The line to say:** "Edit by chat" isn't our moat; others already do it. Ours is local and specialised: Bulgarian, built for venues whose menu changes every day, in every app the owner already uses, with setup in person. (Sources checked 11 October 2026; smaller local players may have been missed.)

**"Why is your AI better?"** Owners don't write commands; they write "banitsa's gone" or send a voice note from a noisy kitchen, with typos, in Bulgarian or English. Examples (deck slide 3):
- "banitsa's gone" → sold out, back by itself at tomorrow's opening.
- "coffees up 10%, round it" → every coffee +10%, rounded, all new prices shown before anything changes.
- "take the cake off" when there are two cakes → it asks "Chocolate or lemon cake?" instead of guessing.
bot2site, the free rival, is a guided bot with buttons and no AI, so it can't take a message like this at all. Don't claim we beat Clawfront's AI: we haven't tested it. Say what ours does.

**"Can't someone just copy this?"** The features, yes; the venues and the partners, no. Each piece (chat editing, Instagram, Google, several apps) can be built by a funded team in weeks, and Bulgarian QR-menu apps already have venues. What protects us: (1) venues that are hard to move once their site, domain, table QR codes, menu history and Instagram run through us; (2) till resellers earning 15% every month, who won't switch to a copycat; (3) depth in one job, venues whose menu changes every day (board photos, price guard, allergens, local price-list rules); (4) local know-how: Bulgarian and Balkan languages, local invoicing, setup in person. That's why we move fast with resellers.

**"How will you expand?"** Bulgaria first, sold directly. Then Greece and the Balkans through **till/POS resellers** (every venue must have a fiscal till, so they know every owner and visit regularly) and restaurant accountants, who keep 15%. Order: Romania + Serbia → Greece + Montenegro → Croatia, Albania, Kosovo → the rest. About 191,000 venues, 8× Bulgaria. **Greece:** the biggest prize (3× Bulgaria, only 43.8% have a website); small Greek firms must start e-invoicing from 2 November 2026, so till resellers and accountants are visiting every venue right now. That's our partner opening. Base case: about 3,050 venues and 601,000 € a year profit before salaries in year 5. No competitor in the region updates sites by chat. **After that: new business types on the same engine** (bakeries, salons, auto services, shops), one type at a time, each with its own template and the changes that business makes every day.

**"Is it profitable?"** Each venue leaves 27 € of 30 €. Without a salary, break-even is 5 venues and year 1 makes about +9,574 € with setup fees. With a 1,000 €/month salary, break-even is 42 venues.

**"What if an owner sends 20 photos a day?"** Only specials-board photos go to the expensive model; dish and gallery photos go to a cheap one, and the 30 € plan has a 150-change limit. A heavy photo user then costs us about 2–10 € a month instead of about 74 €.
