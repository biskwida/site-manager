Site Manager: a website for your café, bar or restaurant that you update by just messaging it on WhatsApp, Messenger or Telegram (Viber is not supported).
Write, send a voice note or a photo of your specials board, e.g. "cappuccino is 3.20 now" or "the lemon cake is sold out until tomorrow". The AI shows you the change and it goes live after one tap. Undo any time. Bulgarian and English.
The same message also updates your Google Maps listing: opening hours, holiday hours, the menu, and a post for today's special. You connect your Google Business profile once; after that your website and Google always say the same thing.
You get a ready site from one of three designs in about 15 minutes (it reads your menu from a photo or PDF), a QR code for the tables, and the menu, hours and specials always up to date.
25 € a month per venue (excl. VAT), setup and hosting included, cancel any month. A cheaper 15 € plan (text and voice only, website only) and a 40 € plan (several locations, your own domain).
All prices are per month, in euros.
