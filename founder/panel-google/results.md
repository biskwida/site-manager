## What would flip a no

- If one message also posted the special to my Instagram and Facebook and worked from Viber, I'd think about it, because then it saves me time instead of adding one more thing.
- Own domain and a full export of my site and menu included even on the cheap plan, so I keep everything if I cancel, and a free first month to see if it actually saves me time.
- If the same message also posted the special to my Instagram and Facebook, and kept Google Maps hours and menu right, so I'd stop updating three places separately.
- A free first month and a Bulgarian phone number with a named person behind it, ideally recommended by another restaurant owner I know.
- Another café owner I know telling me she uses it and it never got her prices wrong, and someone who comes in person to set it up with me.
- Viber support, so I can send the voice note in the same app I already use all day.
- If the same message also updated my menu and prices on Glovo and Wolt, and they moved my existing site over for me without me doing anything.
- A clear promise that my own domain works on the 25 € plan and that if I cancel I keep the domain, can export the site and menu, and my Google profile stays untouched.
- If my manager tried it, said it really saves time on Google hours and the menu, and it could keep my existing site address with nothing to move.
- If it kept my existing site and just handled the daily special on Google Maps, with a free first month so I could see if it actually brings people in at lunch.
- My manager tries it free for a month, actually uses it, and I can see it brought in more customers from Google.
- If the same message also updated my Glovo and Wolt menus, and there was a Bulgarian person I could phone, I'd try the cheap plan.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
