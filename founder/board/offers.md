# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Effort and time are close to zero.** A chat message or voice note, a preview, one tap. The owner already lives in the chat app, so almost no new behaviour is asked. The strongest part of the value equation.
- **Likelihood has early proof.** The product is live and one real venue (Coffique) serves its menu from it. Undo, the change log and auto-revert lower the fear of breaking something.
- **Price signals sit around the list price.** Four quotes (20, 30, 50, 70 €) put 25 € at or below what three of the four named. Against ~6.4 € estimated AI cost per venue, margin looks healthy if that estimate holds.
- **Buyers are easy to reach.** Venues are physical, clustered and visible; the founder already sells door to door.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The pain may not be massive.** An out-of-date website menu is an annoyance for owners who get by on Instagram, Facebook and Google. Small independent venues in one city also score low on spending power. A weak market is why this lens most often passes.
2. **The outcome is not tied to money.** The offer sells "the site stays current"; the owner buys more covers and fewer wrong-price complaints. Nothing is measured or guaranteed, so it drifts toward a commodity "cheap website" comparison.
3. **The stack is thin where customers actually look.** Google hours/pin and Instagram posting are planned, not built, and Viber, the main local messenger, is missing.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which of the four quoting venues will pay now, and why did two value it at 50 € and 70 €?
- What does a wrong menu or wrong hours cost a venue per month?
- How many venues fit the profile in Sofia and Bulgaria? (Unknown.)
- What do competitors and local freelancers charge? (Unknown.)
- What does winning one venue door to door cost? (Unknown.)
- Does the 6.4 € AI cost hold for a venue sending ten messages a day?
- Could one tier at double the price cover Google and Instagram too?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. At least three paying venues (not quotes) within 60 days, one at 40 € or more.
2. Reframe around one measurable outcome ("every place customers look shows the right menu and hours, updated in 30 seconds") with a guarantee, such as the first month refunded if it is not used weekly.
3. Ship Google Business Profile updates before scaling sales; name tiers by outcome, not change counts.
4. Bring back a venue count and a cost-per-venue-won figure from the first 20 visits.
