Public sources, accessed 2026-10-11 unless dated. Eurostat via its API. **ESTIMATE** = my arithmetic or judgement.

## 1. Market size

- **76,728 food & beverage enterprises (NACE I56), 2024**; 76,955 in 2023 ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)).; 519,170 employed, €11.48 bn turnover (2023).
- **81,347 local units, 2024** ([Eurostat sbs_r_nuts2021](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_r_nuts2021/default/table)): **Attica 20,129**; Central Macedonia (Thessaloniki's region) 12,037. No Thessaloniki-city figure found.
- **Independents:** no chain/independent split found; proxy: **61,430 of 76,955 (79.8%) employ 0–9 people** ([Eurostat sbs_sc_ovw](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_sc_ovw/default/table), 2023).
- **Churn** ([Eurostat bd_size](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/bd_size/default/table), updated 2026-10-02): in 2024 there were 7,548 births (9.84%) and 4,538 deaths (5.91%). (2023: 7,132 / 4,377). **ESTIMATE:** closures alone ≈ 0.5% customer churn per month.

|---|---|---|

Greece trails the EU by ~32 points and is falling. **ESTIMATE:** micro venues (80% of the sector) are lower still.

**QR menus:** no adoption statistic found; many Greek vendors exist (section 6).

## 3. Seasonality

- The turnover index for I56 (not seasonally adjusted, 2021=100) ran from **118.3 in Feb 2025 to 249.1 in Aug 2025, a 2.1× swing** ([Eurostat sts_setu_m](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sts_setu_m/default/table)).
- The island regions (South Aegean 6,679, Crete 6,316, Ionian 4,217, North Aegean 2,155) hold **19,367 local units, 23.8% of the national total** (sbs_r_nuts2021, 2024).
- Corfu: seasonal catering closed until Easter ([enimerosi.com](https://www.enimerosi.com/news/74920/Large-number-of-restaurants-closed---even-in-Corfu-Town), Jan 2023). No INSETE/Bank of Greece share of seasonal venues found.
- **ESTIMATE:** ~a quarter of venues close 5–6 months a year and would cancel a monthly plan each autumn.

## 5. Currency, language, legal

- **Currency and language:** EUR; bot and menus must work in Greek. Price lists must be in Greek, other languages optional ([EEA](https://www.eea.gr/arthra-eea/oi-timokatalogoi-se-epixeiriseis-estiasis-anapsyxis/), 2017-11-02, citing ΦΕΚ 2983 Β/30-08-2017, art. 69–71).
- **Price display:** VAT-inclusive prices, shown before ordering and at the entrance; frozen/pre-cooked flags; fines €500 per item, €1,000 for no list.
- **Allergens:** under EU 1169/2011, allergen info on the price list or a prominent sign ([EFET circular 20303](https://www.efet.gr/files/F345_egkyklios_alergiogona.pdf), 2014-12-22).
- **VAT:** B2B services from a non-Greek seller carry no Greek VAT; the customer reverse-charges ([Marosa VAT manual](https://marosavat.com/manual/vat/greece/reverse-charge/)). Standard rate 24%.
- **myDATA:** a foreign issuer need not transmit; the Greek buyer records the invoice ([invoicedataextraction.com](https://invoicedataextraction.com/blog/timologia-exoterikou-mydata-lipti-fpa-e3), secondary source).
- **Mandatory B2B e-invoicing:** starts **2 Nov 2026** for firms with turnover of €1M or less, with a hard cutoff on 1 Feb 2027 ([VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/10/06/greece-moves-smaller-business-e-invoicing-start-to-2-november-2026/), 2026-10-06). If partners **resell** (invoice venues themselves), they carry this. Confirm with a Greek accountant.

## 8. Price level

- Average turnover is **about €149k per enterprise** (€11.48 bn / 76,955, 2023, **ESTIMATE**). 300 € a year is 0.2% of that.
- Greek QR tools: €9–25/mo or €100–150/yr; QR.gr with ordering €36–65 + VAT. 25 € is top of menu-only pricing, justified only by website + chat updates.
- Agency sites start at €300, so 199 € setup is competitive.

**Recommendation (ESTIMATE):** keep **25 €/mo + VAT** list price. Offer an **annual prepay of 240 € (20 €/mo)**, and a **seasonal plan (Apr–Oct, 7 × 25 €)** for islands. Setup **149 €** when a partner onboards.

## 9. Verdict

**Attractiveness: 6/10.**

**Top risks:**
1. Viber-first owners. WhatsApp (27%) is weak; Viber costs about €100 per bot.
2. Seasonality: a 2.1× swing, and about 24% of units are on islands.
3. Crowded cheap QR-menu market (€0–25), and some resellers sell their own menus.

**Top advantages:**
1. Big digital gap: 43.8% have a website vs 76.5% across the EU, and only 33.9% show prices online.
2. A large, micro-dominated market: 76.7k enterprises, 80% with fewer than 10 staff.
3. EUR + reverse charge keep invoicing simple; the e-invoicing rollout gives partners a reason to call every venue now.

**Entry approach:**
- Start in **Athens (Attica, 20.1k units, year-round demand)** through one POS/register reseller and one hospitality accountant.
- Messenger + Telegram, Greek menus with VAT-inclusive prices and allergen/frozen flags.
- Thessaloniki second; islands only on the seasonal plan.
