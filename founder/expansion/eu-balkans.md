**Caveats:**
- The website survey covers only firms with 10 or more employees, and NACE I includes hotels. Hotel website coverage is 99–100% in Croatia and Slovenia (I55, 2025). Micro cafés are therefore far less covered than these figures suggest. Eurostat publishes no Romanian 2025 figure for NACE I.
- Romania's regional local-unit total (24,691) is lower than its enterprise count. The two series differ in coverage.

## Romania

**Market.**
- 29,965 food-service enterprises and 151,815 persons employed (2024). Bucharest-Ilfov has 4,148 local units.
- In 2024 there were 3,729 births and 2,842 deaths, a 9.5% death rate. This is the churn floor.
- The HoReCa association HORA estimated about 33,000 HoReCa units in 2024 and "at least 10% fewer" by now ([Economedia, 2026-01-06](https://economedia.ro/radu-savopol-presedinte-hora-in-2024-existau-aproximativ-33-000-unitati-horeca-in-romania-astazi-cred-ca-sunt-cu-10-mai-putine.html)). Its members report 5–15% lower traffic after the new fiscal measures.
- HoReCa VAT went from 9% to 11% on 1 Aug 2025 ([RHN](https://rhn.ro/en/horeca-industry-will-have-a-vat-rate-of-11-from-august-1/)).

**Digital gap.** 63.8% of NACE I firms had a website in 2023, 12.7 points below the EU average. The largest gap of the three.

**Seasonality.** Low. July/February turnover ratio 1.36x.

**Messaging.**
- WhatsApp leads. Sensor Tower puts WhatsApp at about 12.4M weekly active users on Android alone in Q1 2025 ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q1-android-top-5-communication%20apps-units-ro-6070aae1241bc16eb81f5bab)). It puts Messenger at about 6.1M across all platforms in Q3 2024 ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2024-q3-unified-top-5-communication%20apps-units-ro-6070aae1241bc16eb81f5bab)).
- Messenger's ad reach is 7.30M, or 38.7% of the population ([DataReportal Digital 2026, 2025-11-08](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-romania)).
- No usage-share data for Viber was found. **No Viber bot needed.**

**Currency, language, legal.**
- Menus are priced in RON. The product needs Romanian, plus Hungarian in Transylvania ([doXmenu, 2026-06-10](https://www.doxmenu.com/blog/qr-menu-romania-restaurants/)).
- e-Factura covers businesses established in Romania and foreign businesses registered for Romanian VAT ([EC eInvoicing Romania](https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/sites/display/DIGITAL/eInvoicing+in+Romania)). An unregistered EU seller invoices B2B services without VAT, and the customer reverse-charges ([Marosavat](https://marosavat.com/manual/vat/romania/reverse-charge/)).
- **The catch:** a venue that is not VAT-registered must obtain a special VAT code (art. 317, form D700) before buying EU services, and then pay 21% VAT itself ([ContApp](https://contapp.ro/blog/tva-servicii-ue-non-ue-neplatitori/)).
- **Recommendation:** have the Romanian partner resell and invoice locally. The partner carries e-Factura duties; the venue needs no extra registration. Confirm with a Romanian accountant.

**Competitors (monthly prices):**
- [Egrup QR](https://www.qr.egrup.ro/): 36 lei (€6.74) Standard, 62 lei (€11.61) Pro.
- [MeniuDigital.ro](https://www.meniudigital.ro/): free up to 30 products, then 1,000 RON/yr, about €15.60/mo.
- [meniudigital.restaurant](https://meniudigital.restaurant/) and [Digimeniu](https://digimeniu.ro/): about 69–81 lei/mo (search snippet only, page not opened).
- [doXmenu](https://www.doxmenu.com/blog/qr-menu-romania-restaurants/): €12.99/mo on annual billing.
- The closest substitute is [Logic Industry](https://logicindustry.ro/Creare-Site-Restaurant/Romania): a restaurant site plus monthly updates and social posts for 750 lei/mo (€140), with no minimum term.
- [DigiMenu.ro](https://digimenu.ro/) shows €5/mo in search results, but its page did not render.

**Agency builds:** from 699 RON (€131) at [SprintWeb](https://www.sprintwebstudio.ro/ro/creare-site-restaurante/) (hosting €10/mo) to 2,900 RON (€543) excl. VAT at [Digital Cuisine](https://digitalcuisine.ro/preturi/) (search snippets).

**Delivery platforms:** Glovo, Bolt Food and Wolt. Wolt closed its purchase of Tazz from eMAG on 2025-01-14 ([Wolt](https://press.wolt.com/en-WW/245944-wolt-completes-the-acquisition-of-romanian-local-commerce-platform-tazz/)).

**Partners:**
- POS: [FreyaPOS](https://www.forbes.ro/?p=270229), which self-reports 2,500+ installations (advertorial, 2022-06-17).
- POS: [Ebriza](https://profit.ro/insider/companii/startup-ul-romanesc-ebriza-pentru-digitalizarea-gestiunii-afacerilor-primeste-o-finantare-de-300-000-euro-compania-este-evaluata-la-1-5-milioane-euro-17984046), a cloud HoReCa POS backed by BT Investments.
- Association: [HORA](https://economedia.ro/radu-savopol-presedinte-hora-in-2024-existau-aproximativ-33-000-unitati-horeca-in-romania-astazi-cred-ca-sunt-cu-10-mai-putine.html).
- Agencies (white-label): SprintWeb, Logic Industry.
- Fiscal registers: [Telekom Romania sold Datecs fiscal registers](https://profit.ro/taxe-si-consultanta/telekom-romania-include-in-portofoliul-sau-de-solutii-digitale-pentru-companii-si-case-de-marcat-cu-jurnal-electronic-18929955) (undated).

**Price.** 25 € is above every local QR menu (≤ €15.60) and 6x Egrup.
- **Recommend €19/mo (≈ 99 lei) plus €99 setup.** **ESTIMATE.** It sits just above the top QR menu, since a full website is included, and is about 7x cheaper than Logic Industry.
- After the partner's 25% (€4.75) and the ~€6.4 AI cost, about €7.85/venue remains. Thin: cap AI usage.

**Verdict: 7/10.**
- Risks: a contracting HoReCa market, VAT and invoicing friction, and a low price ceiling.
- Advantages: the largest market (1.7x Bulgaria), the biggest website gap, and WhatsApp-only (no Viber cost).
- **Entry city: Bucharest.**

## Croatia

**Market.**
- 17,784 enterprises and 20,652 local units (2024). City of Zagreb has 3,371; Adriatic Croatia has 11,741 (57%).
- 2024: 1,615 births and 1,275 deaths (7.2%).

**Digital gap.** 74.1% of NACE I firms had a website in 2025, near the EU average. The figure was 55.7% in 2023, so it is volatile.

**Seasonality.** The strongest of the three:
- June to September earned 44.8% of 2025 turnover.
- August turnover is 2.34x February's.
- Coastal venues idle in winter, so expect seasonal churn. **ESTIMATE:** offer a season plan (May–Oct) or a €9 winter pause.

**Messaging.**
- The regulator's survey (HAKOM with 4 Market Research, n=1,000, Nov 2025) names WhatsApp, Viber and Messenger as most used, in that order, with no percentages ([Index.hr/Hina, 2026-01-30](https://www.index.hr/vijesti/clanak/hrvatski-gradjani-najvise-koriste-mobilne-pozive-i-internet/2755682.aspx)).
- WhatsApp has about 1.9M weekly active users ([Sensor Tower Q1 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q1-unified-top-5-communication%20apps-units-hr-6070aae1241bc16eb81f5bab)).
- Viber claimed in 2021 that 80% of smartphone users had it installed (company claim, [tportal](https://www.tportal.hr/tehno/clanak/je-li-viber-u-hrvatskoj-profitirao-od-whatsappove-greske-evo-sto-su-nam-rekli-20210204)).
- **Launch on WhatsApp and Messenger.** Add the €100/mo Viber bot only after about 20 paying venues. At €18.75 net, it takes 6 venues to cover. **ESTIMATE.**

**Currency, language, legal.**
- EUR. The product needs Croatian, plus English, German and Italian for tourists. doXmenu says the coast needs at least four languages ([doXmenu, 2026-06-07](https://www.doxmenu.com/blog/qr-menu-cost-croatia/)).
- Fiscalisation 2.0 (NN 89/2025) has required domestic B2B e-invoices since 2026-01-01. Sellers with no seat in Croatia are out of scope ([VATupdate, 2026-06-21](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/06/21/e-invoicing-in-croatia-a-briefing-document/); [ecosio](https://ecosio.com/en/blog/e-invoicing-croatia-fiscalisation-2-0-e-reporting/)).
- Since 2026, B2C fiscalisation applies to every payment method. That keeps fiscal-register resellers in every venue, which makes them a strong channel.

**Competitors (monthly prices):**
- [Cjenik.net](https://cjenik.net/): €70/yr (€5.83/mo).
- [QR Cjenik](https://qrcjenik.com/): €9 Lite, €16 Pro, €27 Full.
- [Digitalni cjenik](https://digitalni-cjenik.com/): €12/mo (search snippet).
- [doXmenu](https://www.doxmenu.com/blog/qr-menu-cost-croatia/): €12.99/mo.
- [CJENIK.pro](https://cjenik.pro/): €12 to €49/mo (search snippet; page **unreachable**, TLS certificate error).
- [MP WEB](https://mp-web.hr/izrada-web-stranica-za-restorane-i-objekte-brze-hrane/) offers a rented restaurant site at €50 upfront plus €25/mo (search snippet; page did not render). This is the closest analogue to Site Manager.

**Agency builds:**
- [Hortus](https://www.hortus.hr/izrada-web-stranica-cijena/): €530–640 + VAT (prices as of 2026-09-10), maintenance €33–59/mo.
- [Voxern](https://voxern.com/): from €199 (search snippet).
- MP WEB custom multi-page site: up to €1,200 (search snippet).

**Delivery platforms:** Bolt Food left on 2025-10-06. Only Glovo and Wolt remain ([Croatia Week, 2025-09-04](https://www.croatiaweek.com/?p=157538)).

**Partners:**
- [Monri / Remaris](https://monri.com/monri-and-distributors-2025-2025/), a hospitality fiscal register: 40+ partners, 1,000+ locations a year (2025-04-11).
- Its 2024 HoReCa distributor of the year, **Tehno Sistem**.
- Associations: [Udruženje ugostitelja Zagreb](https://www.croatiaweek.com/zagrebs-hospitality-sector-wrestles-with-rising-expenses-and-workforce-shortfall/franz-letica-predsjednik-udrua%c2%beenja-ugostitelja-zagreb/), the Nezavisna udruga ugostitelja, and HOK's hospitality guild ([Zagreb forum](https://zagreb.hr/en/odrzan-forum-zagrebackih-ugostitelja/183695)).
- Agencies: Hortus and Voxern.

**Price.** Local QR menus run €6–27, and MP WEB rents a site for €25/mo. **Keep €25/mo plus €199 setup.** Add a season plan (6 months at €25, then €9 to keep the site live in winter). **ESTIMATE.**

**Verdict: 6/10.**
- Risks: seasonal churn, Viber pressure, and many cheap QR menus.
- Advantages: EUR pricing, a regulator-confirmed WhatsApp lead, and a dense fiscal-register reseller channel.
- **Entry city: Zagreb**, which is busy year-round. Add Split before the 2027 season.

## Slovenia

**Market.**
- 8,688 enterprises and 9,176 local units (2024). Western Slovenia, which includes Ljubljana, has 4,868. No Ljubljana-only figure was found in Eurostat.
- 2024: 924 births and 734 deaths (8.5%).
- Highest wages of the three: labour cost per employee was €17.0k in 2024, against €12.3k in Bucharest-Ilfov (sbs_r_nuts2021).

**Digital gap.** Small. 87.5% of NACE I firms had a website in 2025, so the pitch is "keep it current by chat", not "get a website".

**Seasonality.** Moderate. August turnover is 1.50x February's.

**Messaging.**
- WhatsApp had more than 645K weekly active users, against Viber at about 154K ([Sensor Tower Q2 2024](https://sensortower.com/blog/2024-q2-unified-top-5-social%20networking-units-si-600b30a6241bc16eb80bb51d)).
- N1 names WhatsApp, Messenger and Viber as the most widespread ([N1](https://n1info.si/novice/svet/izklopite-funkcijo-na-viberju-ki-vas-lahko-veliko-stane/)).
- No Slovenian survey with shares was found. [wmtips](https://www.wmtips.com/technologies/instant-messaging/country/si/) returned **403**.

**Currency, language, legal.**
- EUR. The product needs Slovenian.
- Fiscal registers (davčne blagajne) have been mandatory since 2016 ([Žurnal24 advertorial](https://www.zurnal24.si/uporabno/davcna-blagajna-za-gostince-je-kljucna-za-vas-posel-406169)).
- B2B e-invoicing becomes mandatory from 2028-01-01 for entities in the Slovenian register, with no tax reporting ([KPMG, 2025-10](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/10/slovenia-mandatory-e-invoicing-b2b-transactions.html)). It does not apply to a foreign seller.

**Competitors (monthly prices):**
- [emenu.si](https://www.emenu.si/): €9 / €20 + VAT, billed yearly with a 12-month minimum.
- [SCAN MENU](https://www.scanmenu.si/): €250 the first year, then €200/yr, with a 24-month minimum.
- [enjoi.si](https://enjoi.si/): free menus.
- [Revolver](https://www.revolver.si/online-cenik-s-qr-kodo/): €60 + VAT one-off (search snippet).

**Agency builds:**
- Market average €1,400, typical range €1,300–2,200 ([primerjam.si, "cene 2026"](https://www.primerjam.si/cene/izdelava-spletnih-strani/cena-izdelave-spletne-strani)).
- [OptiBull](https://www.optibull.si/izdelava-spletnih-strani-cenik/): €790; [Spletnik](https://spletnik.si/izdelava-spletne-strani/cenik/): €1,499 (search snippets).

**Delivery platforms:** Glovo left on 2024-05-10, leaving Wolt dominant ([24ur](https://www.24ur.com/novice/gospodarstvo/glovo-odhaja-iz-slovenije.html)).

**Partners:**
- Associations: [TGZS at GZS](https://tax-fin-lex.si/Home/Novica/20733) and the [OZS hospitality section](https://www.slovenia.info/sl/novinarsko-sredisce/novice/28697-ozs-in-sto-krepita-sodelovanje-pri-projektu-gostilna-slovenija), which runs the Gostilna Slovenija label.
- POS sellers: Shopsy.si.
- QR providers that could resell: emenu.si and SCAN MENU.
- No current, named POS reseller list for HoReCa was found. Monri's regional sites suggest a Slovenian presence, but this is unverified.

**Price.** Agency builds cost the most here and wages are highest, so €25 is affordable. Local menu SaaS runs only €9–21, however. **Keep €25/mo plus €199 setup.** **ESTIMATE.**

**Verdict: 5/10.**
- Risks: the smallest market (0.45x Bulgaria), most venues already have a site, and a thin partner channel.
- Advantages: purchasing power, EUR, and WhatsApp dominance.
- **Entry city: Ljubljana.**
